BARANI S — PORTFOLIO WEBSITE

FILES
- index.html: complete responsive website (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in one file)
- assets/README.txt: instructions for adding your resume PDF

RUN LOCALLY
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open the barani-portfolio folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in your browser.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. In index.html, replace YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with your email address in both the contact link and mailto form handler.
2. Replace YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME with your GitHub username.
3. Replace [Add your university name] and [Add your expected graduation year].
4. Update project links to your actual repositories and live demos. Remove any project that is not yours or not yet built.
5. Put your resume PDF at assets/resume.pdf.
6. Update skills, experience, and project details to match your real experience.

NOTE
The contact form opens the visitor's default email application. It does not send messages through a server.
